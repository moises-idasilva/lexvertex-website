Brazilian and foreign companies

Four practice areas

Each matter is handled under Brazilian law, for local companies and for foreign firms setting up in Brazil.

  1. Corporate law

    Governance, mergers, acquisitions and corporate compliance, from the articles of association to the closing documents.

  2. Intellectual property

    Trademark and patent filings with INPI, enforcement against copies, and litigation when a right is infringed.

  3. Litigation support

    Strategy and representation in civil, commercial and labour disputes, from the first notice to the court's decision.

  4. Compliance and regulatory affairs

    Compliance programmes and risk reviews written for your sector, and kept current as Brazilian rules change.
